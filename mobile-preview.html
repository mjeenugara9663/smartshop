🛡️ Shopkeeper & admin controls

The interactive prototype includes a shopkeeper studio, offer approval, a one-published-change-per-day demo limit, and a basic activity audit.

  • Product names, quantities and structured offer fields only
  • No customer login, payment processing or chat-history database
  • Orders open a prepared WhatsApp message; customers press Send themselves
  • Production enforcement requires a secured backend before any public use
